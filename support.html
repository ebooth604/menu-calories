Support

Need help or found a problem? Open a support request and we'll reply there, usually within two business days. Please don't include personal information.

Common questions

How accurate are the calories?

They are estimates made from the menu text. When a menu prints its own calories, those are used instead. Treat the numbers as a guide, not a measurement.

I paid. How do I get my unlock on a new iPhone?

Open the paywall in Menu Calories and tap Restore Purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Why can't I post, comment or rate?

Sharing needs an iCloud account. Sign in to iCloud in Settings, then accept the community rules in Menu Calories.

How do I report a post or block someone?

Use the report or block option on any post, comment or profile. Content reported by several people is hidden automatically, and reports are reviewed within 24 hours.

How do I delete what I've shared?

In Menu Calories, open Account and choose Delete My Shared Data. This removes your profile, posts, comments, ratings, reports and shared scans.
