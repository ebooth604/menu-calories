Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 October 2026

Menu Calories is designed so that most of what you do stays on your iPhone. This policy explains what stays on your device, what is shared when you choose to share it, and the choices you have.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Menu photos you take or pick, and the text read from them. Menus are read and estimated on your device by Apple's on-device model.
  • Your saved menus, food log, daily goals and Menu Score history.

We don't receive this information. It is removed when you delete the app.

What is shared, only if you choose to

Menu Calories has optional community features that use Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) public database. Nothing is shared unless you use them.

  • Shared scans: the restaurant, the dish names and their estimates. No photo, name, email or your location.
  • Posts: menu photos you choose to post, with faces blurred and location data removed on your iPhone first, plus the dish estimates.
  • Comments and star ratings you write.
  • Your public profile: the nickname, photo, bio, home city and dietary goal you choose to add.
  • Reports you file about posts, comments or profiles.

CloudKit tags each shared record with a random identifier unique to this app. It is not your name, email or Apple Account, and other people can't use it to look you up. Shared items can be linked to each other and to your nickname if you also post publicly.

Location

If you allow location access, your location is used on your iPhone to find nearby restaurants. When you browse Explore, only a rounded location (about 10 km) is sent to look up restaurants. Your location is not stored.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is processed by Apple. We don't receive your payment details.

What we don't do

  • No advertising identifiers, no tracking across apps or websites, and no selling of data.
  • No analytics or third-party SDKs.

Restaurants may pay for a labeled "Sponsored" listing in Explore. This uses no information about you.

Your choices

  • Don't use the community features, and nothing leaves your iPhone except the rounded Explore location lookup.
  • Delete everything you've shared at any time: Account → Delete My Shared Data.
  • Block other users; blocks are stored in your own private iCloud database.

Children

Menu Calories is not directed to children under 13.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open a request. We'll update this page if our practices change.
